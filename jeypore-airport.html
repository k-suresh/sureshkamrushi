Airport at Jeypore Odisha, India

April 19, 2016

Jeypore Airport: Connecting the Heart of Koraput
Nestled in the scenic Koraput district of Odisha, Jeypore Airport serves as a vital gateway to one of India's most picturesque tribal regions. It is located about 3 km from Jeypore city and operates under the Airports Authority of India. Originally built in 1962, the airport was established alongside the nearby Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) facility. Over the years, significant investments have modernized its runway, terminal, and supporting infrastructure. In 2022, the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) approved commercial flight operations from the airport. Today, IndiaOne Air connects Jeypore with Bhubaneswar and Visakhapatnam through regional flights.

The airport plays a key role in improving accessibility, tourism, and economic growth in southern Odisha. Its development supports the government's regional connectivity initiatives and strengthens links between remote communities and major cities. As passenger traffic continues to grow, Jeypore Airport is emerging as an important transportation hub for the region.
